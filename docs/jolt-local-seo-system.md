# Jolt Local SEO Page System

## Strategy Summary

Jolt should scale local SEO by publishing useful pages with distinct intent, not by swapping city and industry names into the same copy. The pages should help local service businesses recognize website friction and request a Free Website Teardown.

The system starts with service, problem, location, and industry pages. City plus industry pages should only be published when there is enough unique local and category context to avoid doorway-style content.

## URL Structure

- City pages: `/locations/bloomington-normal-il`
- Industry pages: `/industries/roofers`
- City plus industry pages: `/locations/peoria-il/med-spas`
- Service pages: `/services/free-website-teardown`
- Problem pages: `/website-problems/website-looks-bad-on-mobile`

## Page Type Hierarchy

1. Homepage
2. Service pages
3. Central Illinois location hub
4. Priority city pages
5. Priority industry pages
6. Select city plus industry pages
7. Problem-based education pages

## Internal Linking Strategy

- City pages link to relevant services, nearby locations, priority industries, and problem pages.
- Industry pages link to relevant services, priority city pages, and problem pages.
- City plus industry pages link to parent city, parent industry, teardown, and the best-fit package.
- Service pages link to problem pages, top industries, and the Free Website Teardown.
- Problem pages link to teardown, Core Website Refresh, Starter Landing Page, and relevant industry pages.

Use natural anchors such as "website refresh for roofers", "Free Website Teardown", and "Bloomington-Normal website refresh services". Avoid repeated exact-match anchor stuffing.

Phase 1 should contain 10 to 15 pages:

- `/locations/central-illinois`

- `/locations/peoria-il`
- `/industries/contractors`
- `/industries/roofers`

- `/services/core-website-refresh`
- `/website-problems/website-looks-bad-on-mobile`
- `/website-problems/website-not-getting-quote-requests`

Phase 2 should contain 25 to 40 pages:

- Add Champaign-Urbana, Springfield, Decatur, Morton, Washington, and East Peoria if useful local context exists.
- Add landscapers, cleaners, remodelers, auto shops, clinics, junk removal, and groomers.
- Add a small number of city plus industry pages only where search intent and context are strong.

Phase 3 should contain 60 to 100 pages:

- Scale only after collecting teardown examples, screenshots, real photos, Search Console data, and approved proof.
- Merge or noindex pages that overlap too much.

## Pages To Delay

Do not publish city plus industry pages for every combination. Delay pages like:

- `/locations/pekin-il/roofers`
- `/locations/lincoln-il/med-spas`
- `/locations/eureka-il/junk-removal`
- `/locations/champaign-il/groomers`

Publish only when the page has unique local context, category norms, real examples, photos, or proof.

## Doorway And Scaled Content Rules

- No page publishes without a distinct search intent.
- At least 30 to 40 percent of the page must be meaningfully unique.
- Do not invent testimonials, clients, project outcomes, awards, rankings, or guarantees.
- Do not overuse "near me", "best", or "top-rated".
- Do not create pages for small towns unless there is enough useful local information.
- City plus industry pages must answer a narrower question than either parent page.
- Use noindex for drafts and thin pages waiting on proof.
- If two pages answer the same intent, consolidate.

## Reusable Page Templates

Every page type should include:

- SEO title formula
- Meta description formula
- H1 formula
- Above-the-fold section
- Local or industry-specific pain point section
- What Jolt fixes section
- Package fit section
- Proof or trust section
- FAQ section
- CTA section
- Internal links
- Schema recommendations
- Required unique inputs before publishing

## Required Unique Inputs

Every generated page must define:

- City or industry
- Specific customer pain points
- Relevant service examples
- Local context or industry context
- Real photo ideas
- Real proof, if available
- Local competitors or category norms, if relevant
- Search intent
- Primary CTA
- Internal links
- FAQs unique to the page

If these inputs are too thin, mark the page "do not publish yet" and collect more information.

## Publishing Workflow

1. Draft the page from the reusable data model.
2. Confirm the search intent is distinct.
3. Add unique city, industry, service, or problem context.
4. Add real proof only when available.
5. Add real photo ideas.
6. Review all claims for accuracy.
7. Confirm internal links support the hierarchy.
8. Check mobile layout.
9. Publish.
10. Review Search Console queries monthly and improve, merge, or noindex pages as needed.

## Human Review Checklist

- Does this page answer a distinct search intent?
- Would a real business owner find it useful without Google?
- Are all claims accurate and provable?
- Does it avoid fake proof and generic local SEO filler?
- Are FAQs unique to the page?
- Is the Free Website Teardown CTA clear?
- Are internal links useful?
- Is the page strong enough to publish, or should it wait for more proof?
